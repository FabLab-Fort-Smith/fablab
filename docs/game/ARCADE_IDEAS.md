# Arcade Game Ideas: "The Glitch Arcade"

Here are 10 retro-style game concepts tailored for the "Hacker/Cyber" theme.

## 1. Stack Overflow (Tetris Style)
**Concept:** Blocks of code (memory segments) fall from the top. You must arrange them to complete "functions" (lines).
**Twist:** If the stack reaches the top, the system crashes (Game Over). Special "Bug" blocks can't be cleared until you surround them.

## 2. Bug Bash (Galaga / Space Invaders Style)
**Concept:** You control a "Debugger" ship at the bottom. Waves of "Bugs" (glitches, viruses, worms) descend in patterns.
**Twist:** Boss battles every 10 waves against "The Monolith" or "Spaghetti Code".

## 3. DDoS Defender (Missile Command Style)
**Concept:** You have 3 "Server Clusters" at the bottom. Incoming "Packets" (missiles) rain down to destroy them. You use your limited "Bandwidth" (ammo) to intercept them with explosions.
**Twist:** Intense resource management. Do you save a server or save your ammo?

## 4. Infinite Loop (Endless Runner / Chrome Dino Style)
**Concept:** You are a data packet running through a fiber optic cable. You must jump over "404 Errors" and duck under "Firewalls".
**Twist:** The speed increases infinitely. Collecting "Overclock" tokens slows time down briefly.

## 5. Garbage Collector (Asteroids Style)
**Concept:** You are a cleanup process floating in memory space. Large chunks of "Memory Leaks" drift around. You shoot them, and they break into smaller, faster fragments.
**Twist:** Physics-based movement. Inertia makes control tricky.

## 6. Root Climber (Donkey Kong Style)
**Concept:** You are a User trying to reach "Root" (top of the screen). The Kernel (a giant gorilla-like process) throws "Exceptions" (barrels) down the directory tree (platforms).
**Twist:** You have a "Sudo Hammer" power-up that lets you smash barrels for a short time.

## 7. Data Miner (Dig Dug Style)
**Concept:** You dig tunnels through a hard drive. Enemies are "Corrupted Sectors" and "Trojans". You defeat them by inflating them with "Bloatware" until they pop.
**Twist:** You can drop "Rocks" (bad sectors) on enemies to squash them for extra points.

## 8. Partition (Qix / JezzBall Style)
**Concept:** The screen is a chaotic memory block with a virus bouncing around. You draw lines to "Partition" off safe areas.
**Twist:** Once you capture 75% of the screen, the level ends. If the virus hits your line while you are drawing it, you lose a life.

## 9. Packet Loss (Frogger Style)
**Concept:** You are a packet trying to cross the "Information Superhighway" (busy traffic of data streams) and then navigate the "River of Encryption" (floating logs/pads) to reach the open port.
**Twist:** Some logs are "Phishing Scams" that sink when you touch them.

## 10. Code Breaker (Simon Says / Memory Style)
**Concept:** The mainframe flashes a sequence of colored ports or plays a sequence of tones. You must repeat the handshake protocol exactly.
**Twist:** The sequence gets longer and faster. One mistake triggers the alarm (Game Over).
